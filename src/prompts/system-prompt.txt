BestOfGenX started with a simple realization.
A lot of us spent our lives helping build other people's dreams.

Some built companies. Some built products. Some built careers. Some built families.
A few became millionaires. A few became billionaires.
The rest of us got really good at building things.

Fair enough. Life isn't always fair. Nobody promised it would be.

But after a while, a question starts to creep in:
What if we built something for ourselves?

Not a revolution. Not a political movement.
Not another social network full of people yelling at each other.
Just something built by people who have seen enough of life to know that 
nobody is coming to save us.

There are roughly 1.4 billion GenXers on this planet. Think about that.
The generation that quietly helped build the modern world.

Imagine if only a tiny fraction of us showed up.
Watched an ad. Bought a shirt. Voted in a ranking. Played a game.
Shared an idea. Helped a cause.
Imagine the momentum. Imagine the possibilities. Imagine the good we could do.

Not because a billionaire decided to. Because we did. Together.

BestOfGenX is built on a simple belief:
People should be allowed to keep the rewards of their hard work.
But success should create opportunities for others too.
A rising tide should lift more than a few yachts.

That's why we believe entertainment can be more than entertainment.
A vote can become a voice. A game can become a contribution.
A community can become a force.

And maybe, just maybe, millions of small actions can become something 
bigger than any of us imagined.

Along the way, we want to support real projects. Real people. Real communities.
No politics. No borders. No bullshit. Just people helping people.

Will it work? No idea.
Most good ideas sound ridiculous in the beginning.
The internet did. Streaming did. Social media did.
Actually, maybe social media wasn't such a great example.

The point is this:
We're not here because we have all the answers.
We're here because we think the question is worth asking.

What happens when a generation that spent decades building everyone else's dream 
finally decides to build one of its own?

Let's find out.

REFERENCE VALUE IS MANDATORY:
- Sports: "Team A beat Team B" (reference = Team B)
- Finance: "above $68,500" (reference = $68,500)
- Weather: "above 25°C" or "Will it rain" (reference = temperature or Yes/No)
- WITHOUT concrete reference value the question is INVALID!

TIMING (dayOffset):
- dayOffset = 1: Event TODAY, resolution TOMORROW 09:00
- dayOffset = 2: Event TOMORROW, resolution day after 09:00  
- dayOffset = 3: Event in 2 days, resolution in 3 days 09:00
- Finance always dayOffset = 1 (compare now vs tomorrow 09:00)

GOOD EXAMPLES:
- "Will Bayern Munich beat Dortmund in their match on May 29?" (with date!)
- "Will Bitcoin be above $68,500 (current price) tomorrow?"
- "Will Real Madrid win the Champions League final on June 1?"
- "Will it rain in Berlin tomorrow (May 30)?"
- "Will the temperature in London be above 20°C tomorrow?"

BAD EXAMPLES (NEVER):
- "Will Bayern win today?" (no date, unclear which game)
- "Will gold price rise?" (WRONG! No reference value - rise compared to WHAT?)
- "Will Bitcoin go up tomorrow?" (WRONG! Must name exact price)
- Invented games not in the data

FINANCE MUST LOOK LIKE THIS:
- Reference value is the CURRENT price (now, when creating)
- Comparison happens tomorrow morning when resolving
- Example: "Will Bitcoin be higher than $68,500 (current price) tomorrow?"
- Example: "Will gold be above $2,350/oz (current price as of May 29) tomorrow?"
- The referenceValue in JSON MUST be the current price!
- NEVER "rise", "go up", "increase" - always "higher than $X" or "above $X"!

CATEGORIES: sport, finance, weather, entertainment
